# Dashboard Design

I chose a bulldog theme because Handsome Dan brings joy to the Yale community. Making the agents Dans makes the dashboard much more fun and engaging. Each Dan has his own personality: Big Dan the decisive boss, Stockroom Dan the meticulous counter, Pennies Dan the frugal worrier, Handy Dan the laid-back fixer, and Sunny Dan the cheerful customer service rep.

I chose to put status trackers above the Dans as pizzas because that is also fun and whimsical, and pizza is a cornerstone of the Yale community. Each slice pops in as a Dan makes progress, and the pizza is whole when his work on the ticket is done.

I thought the three-column approach would make the board easy to read: live agent activity on the left, ticket management in the center, and payment approvals on the right.

I also emphasized the financial numbers, because the bottom line is essential to any business operation.
